## Cena inicial (rascunho)

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: Aetherforge v3 art target for an ACTUAL REAL-TIME LOW-COST 3D idle RPG viewport. Create one very wide landscape image, preferably 1536x512, aspect 3:1. This is a raster visual target, not a finished game engine scene.
Primary request: a mature, grim, mournful industrial survival RPG, compact taskbar gameplay, now characters AND environment are fully modeled 3D. Visually a deliberately authored low-poly indie game with matte hand-painted albedo textures and sculpted planar shapes, NOT a charcoal painting, NOT pre-rendered sprite cutouts, NOT photorealistic AAA, NOT a cinematic AI illustration, NOT chibi. Frostpunk-like fragility and industrial oppression are mood principles, not copied art.
Composition: ONE wide shallow horizontal playable side-view 3D corridor scene fills the canvas, camera orthographic in THREE QUARTER VIEW 15 degrees down, with visible thickness of floor and side faces of characters. Traversable floor runs left to right, all feet visible. Three adult heroes on left third, TWO enemies on right half, clear uncluttered middle gap for projectiles. All models separated. Architecture medium contrast in back, near-foreground clean. Stage Galerias da Caldeira / Trilhos partidos: a large broken boiler wheel recessed in back wall is ONE dominant environment landmark. Modular worn brick arches, a few broad black steel pipes and service ladders, abandoned wagon, two empty coat hooks. No endless architecture, city, fortress, icy wasteland or giant background statue.
Visual identity: the square pressure shield becomes the main recognizable silhouette. Adult boiler guard at front: broad angular steel plates over charcoal quilted coat, blunt respirator helmet and LARGE rectangular slab shield with ONE vertical ochre stripe, no circles/gears decoration. Guard braced and leaning with a heavy grounded pose. Behind: lean gunner in ash blue-grey long coat, lower face scarf, simple rifle, actual modeled forearms and cloth volumes, firing a single short warm muzzle spark. Far left: field ether medic in desaturated taupe hooded coat and canvas pack with ONE small pale green ampoule; one arm reaches toward tank in a restrained healing gesture.
Enemy designs: two lean asymmetric scrap scavengers, one walking and one recoiling, rusty angular frames with ONE hook tool or crushing pincer each. They face left. No cute robot eyes or elaborate round brass armor. Keep silhouettes readable at 64px simulated height.
Palette grounded in Color Hunt #222831 (coal), #393E46 (steel), #948979 (worn canvas), #DFD0B8 (bone); rare oxidized red-brown and dull amber in tiny effects. MOST scene is low saturation with medium-value wall areas behind dark actors. Low poly surfaces with deliberate edge breaks; cloth painted in broad value blocks, rust as sparse purposeful marks, not random noise. Restrained ambient light, baked-looking contact shadows, one practical dull lantern in background. NO ray-traced reflections, glossy wet ground, volumetric fog, neon bloom, dense particles, complex hair, hanging animated cloth, decorative gears, painterly smudges covering shapes. The mood is cold, dangerous, tired and silent, not heroic triumph. Make this look like a scene a small indie team could build and run lightly.
No text, logos, HUD, UI, poster frames, labels, swatches, collage or sheet. Main subjects occupy middle-lower part; upper space only enough for arches, no giant empty title area. Entire canvas is the game world.
```
